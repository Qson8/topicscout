> Coverage note: this example judges **all 18 titles** of that day's dual
> list. The dual list is the author's own second-order curation (18 items

> For any single platform's original chart, the rule is excerpt-only.

## The profile (judgments are meaningless without one)

**壹念手记 (Yinian Notes)** — a personal blog, four columns: 生活 (daily
life) / 人文 (people & ideas) / 国风 (traditional culture) / 技术
(technology). One article per column per day (daily capacity 4; the 国风 slot is
filled by the standing daily-verse column). Audience: general readers. No-go zones: no medical
efficacy claims, no investment advice or price predictions, policy &
breaking-news topics pass a human-confirmation gate before publishing,
religious material in cultural-appreciation framing only. Tags come from a
closed list of 8 — no inventing new tags mid-run.
